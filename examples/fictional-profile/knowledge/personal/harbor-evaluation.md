# Interview question

How was the Harbor localization prototype evaluated?

# Approved account

I built repeatable routes through a test warehouse and compared estimated robot paths with surveyed checkpoints. The evaluation exposed instability near reflective loading bays, so I added scenario labels instead of hiding those runs in an aggregate score. That made the limitation visible and gave the next experiment a concrete target.

# Evidence boundaries

- Harbor and its results are fictional.
- The account does not claim production deployment.
